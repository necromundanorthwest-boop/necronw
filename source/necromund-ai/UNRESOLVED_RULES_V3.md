# Version 3 Player-Confirmed Rules

The supplied materials do not establish enough exact rules text to automate the following safely. Version 3 therefore asks the player to confirm them:

- Current action legality and affordability under the official rules
- Group Activation eligibility, participant order, and initiator action cost
- Inspiring eligibility and timing
- Overwatch reaction timing
- Status-specific action availability
- Blast and Template placement or geometry
- Reckless targeting restrictions
- Measurements, line of sight, dice, damage, and scenario-specific mechanics

This is intentional: the adviser chooses among player-confirmed options instead of inventing mechanics.
